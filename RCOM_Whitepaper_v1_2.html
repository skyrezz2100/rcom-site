03 Reserve policy and future releases

The adopted approach retains the personal allocation, discloses reserves, and considers incremental liquidity additions as trading demand develops. Reserve wallet labels reflect the treasury and community programs designations. Specific spending or release commitments are not established by these labels.

Version 1.1's 200 million community budget and approximately 249.48 million staged-sale budget are historical proposals, not current balance commitments. No giveaway, sale, or release date is announced here. Before future releases, disclose amounts, purposes, recipients or eligibility, timing, settlement, and use of proceeds. Report actual transactions and remaining balances after execution.

04 Liquidity and risks

The founder retains withdrawal access to the Raydium WSOL–RCOM pool. Pool holdings are not tokens distributed to independent owners. Matched deposits may reduce price impact but do not create buyers or confer exchange verification.

RCOM may lose all value. Founder concentration, liquidity withdrawals, key compromise, network or contract failures, and loss of interest can harm holders. Disabled authorities are not a security audit. The founder allocation is transferable; no 24-month vesting contract has been implemented.
