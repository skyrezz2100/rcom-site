02 Founder allocation and wallet control

The founder has designated 50,000,000 RCOM in the wallet below as the personal allocation: exactly 5% of the original one-billion-token supply. The allocation is not locked or vested.

428,082,028 RCOM are locked in Streamflow until 28 August 2028 at 04:00 UTC. The reserve releases all at once, with no early releases. The contract is immutable: it cannot be canceled or transferred. View the public lock certificate.

Treasury and community-program balances were consolidated into the Liquidity/burn wallet before the lock was funded. The founder's personal allocation remains separate and unlocked. The locked reserve's recipient is the founder-controlled Solflare wallet; locking restricts access until the release date and does not distribute ownership to independent holders.

03 Reserve policy and future releases

The project reserve of 428,082,028 RCOM becomes available to the recipient wallet all at once on 28 August 2028 at 04:00 UTC. This is a single-date token lock, not annual vesting. No public sale or automatic distribution to holders is scheduled by this contract.

Earlier proposals for annual releases or community distribution are superseded by this funded reserve lock. Before any distribution after unlock, disclose its amount, purpose, recipients or eligibility, timing, and use of proceeds.

04 Liquidity and risks

The founder retains withdrawal access to the Raydium WSOL–RCOM pool. Pool holdings are not tokens distributed to independent owners. Matched deposits may reduce price impact but do not create buyers or confer exchange verification.

RCOM may lose all value. Founder concentration, liquidity withdrawals, key compromise, network or contract failures, and loss of interest can harm holders. Disabled authorities are not a security audit. The founder personal allocation remains transferable and unlocked. The project reserve is subject to the separate Streamflow lock described above. Smart-contract risk remains, and the scheduled release can increase available supply. The reserve lock does not guarantee a price increase or removal of platform risk warnings.
