04 / QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Comradism. Trust. Unity.

What is RCOM?

RED COMRADE is a Solana meme token for Earthlings, using satire to explore scarcity, ownership, and who gets a share. It represents comradism, trust, and unity: standing together, building openly, and welcoming others.

What does holding RCOM provide?

You own a transferable token and can express participation in the community. Holding RCOM does not grant company ownership, voting rights, income, a claim on the locked reserve, or ownership of the pool's SOL. Its market value depends on demand.

What is locked, and why?

428,082,028 RCOM are locked until August 28, 2028 at 04:00 UTC, with one release and no early cancellation. The lock is a concrete commitment to earning trust: it restricts the founder's access to the reserve while giving the community time to develop. Verify the reserve lock.

Who controls the remaining tokens and liquidity?

The founder controls the project's unlocked wallets, including the 50,000,000 RCOM founder allocation, and the wallet that receives the reserve at unlock. Other holders control their own tokens. The founder also controls the project's Raydium liquidity position and can withdraw it. The reserve lock does not lock pool liquidity.

What are the limitations?

RCOM is an early-stage, speculative meme project. Thin liquidity can cause sharp price moves and make larger sales difficult. Project control remains concentrated, and liquidity withdrawals can reduce trading depth. The reserve lock does not determine how tokens will be used after release; any future distribution plan will be communicated separately. There is no guaranteed adoption, listing, or return, and you can lose the full amount spent.
